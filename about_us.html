About the System

The Cebu Technological University (CTU) BSIT department's student profiling system is designed to streamline the management and organization of student data. It provides a centralized platform for tracking academic records, personal information, and progress, ensuring an efficient and seamless experience for both students and administrators. With this system, CTU aims to enhance transparency, improve accessibility, and empower the BSIT department with the tools needed for better decision-making and student support.
